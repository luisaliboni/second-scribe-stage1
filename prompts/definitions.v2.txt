- experienced: the participant says this happened to them, or existed for them.
- An attempt the participant made (applying, asking, a visit they went through) counts as experienced even if it failed or led nowhere; the thing they were trying to get counts as experienced only if they say they got it; if they wanted or needed it and did not get it, it is wished.
- wished: the participant says they wanted or needed this, or that it should have existed for them, and does not say that it happened or existed for them.
- Everything experienced or wished is part of this person's journey. Not part of it: something the participant says did not happen to them, happened only to someone else, or only might have happened, unless they wanted or needed it themselves (then it is wished).
