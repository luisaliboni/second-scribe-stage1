[I] Thank you for talking with me today. Where did the process start for you?
[P] It started when I went to the town office to ask what I needed for the new shed.
[I] What did they tell you there?
[P] They gave me the permit form and said to bring a drawing of the site.
[I] And after you handed in the form?
[P] I waited about three weeks. Then a letter came saying the drawing needed the distance to the fence.
[I] Was there anything you expected to happen that did not?
[P] Nobody told me about the fire inspection. I wished someone had explained that step before I started building.
[I] How did it end?
[P] The inspector came, I paid the fee, and the permit was signed the same week.
